Venus

Venus is the hottest planet in our solar system, despite not being the closest to the Sun. Its thick atmosphere of carbon dioxide traps heat in a runaway greenhouse effect.

  • Type: Terrestrial Planet
  • Distance from Sun: 108.2 million km
  • Diameter: 12,104 km
  • Orbital Period: 225 Earth days
  • Moons: None
  • Surface Temp: ~470°C
  • Atmosphere: CO₂ & Sulfuric Acid clouds

Venus rotates backwards compared to most planets — the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
