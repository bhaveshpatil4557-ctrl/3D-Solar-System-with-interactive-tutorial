Earth

Earth is our home planet and the only known world in the universe confirmed to support life. Its liquid water, breathable atmosphere, and stable climate make it uniquely habitable.

  • Type: Terrestrial Planet
  • Distance from Sun: 149.6 million km
  • Diameter: 12,742 km
  • Orbital Period: 365.25 days
  • Moons: 1 (The Moon)
  • Surface Temp: −88°C to 58°C
  • Atmosphere: Nitrogen (78%) & Oxygen (21%)

About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water — a key ingredient that sustains all known life.
