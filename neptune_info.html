Neptune

Neptune is the farthest planet from the Sun and the windiest in the solar system. Its deep blue color comes from methane gas in the atmosphere absorbing red light.

  • Type: Ice Giant
  • Distance from Sun: 4.5 billion km
  • Diameter: 49,244 km
  • Orbital Period: ~165 Earth years
  • Moons: 16 known (incl. Triton)
  • Surface Temp: −214°C
  • Wind Speed: Up to 2,100 km/h

Triton, Neptune's largest moon, orbits in the opposite direction of Neptune's rotation — suggesting it was captured from the Kuiper Belt.
