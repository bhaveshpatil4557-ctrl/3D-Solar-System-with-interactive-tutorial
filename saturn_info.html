Saturn

Saturn is the jewel of the solar system, famous for its spectacular ring system made of ice and rock. It is the least dense planet — it would float on water!

  • Type: Gas Giant
  • Distance from Sun: 1.43 billion km
  • Diameter: 116,460 km
  • Orbital Period: ~29.5 Earth years
  • Moons: 146 known (incl. Titan)
  • Surface Temp: −139°C (cloud tops)
  • Rings: Made of ice particles & rock debris

Titan, Saturn's largest moon, has rivers and lakes of liquid methane — the only place besides Earth with stable liquid on the surface.
