Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a striking blue-green color caused by methane in its atmosphere. Uniquely, it rotates on its side — likely due to a massive collision long ago.

  • Type: Ice Giant
  • Distance from Sun: 2.87 billion km
  • Diameter: 50,724 km
  • Orbital Period: ~84 Earth years
  • Moons: 27 known (named after Shakespeare characters)
  • Surface Temp: −197°C
  • Axial Tilt: 97.77° (rotates on its side)

Uranus has 13 known rings, and its magnetic field is tilted 60° from its rotational axis — one of the most unusual in the solar system.
