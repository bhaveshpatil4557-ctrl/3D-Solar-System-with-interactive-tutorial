Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system — so massive that all other planets could fit inside it. Its iconic Great Red Spot is a storm that has raged for hundreds of years.

  • Type: Gas Giant
  • Distance from Sun: 778.5 million km
  • Diameter: 139,820 km
  • Orbital Period: ~12 Earth years
  • Moons: 95 known (incl. Ganymede, Europa)
  • Surface Temp: −108°C (cloud tops)
  • Famous Feature: Great Red Spot storm

Jupiter's magnetic field is the strongest of any planet — about 20,000× stronger than Earth's.
