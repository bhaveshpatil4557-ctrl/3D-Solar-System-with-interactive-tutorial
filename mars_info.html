Mars

Mars, the Red Planet, is a cold desert world with the largest volcano and longest canyon in the solar system. Scientists believe it once had liquid water on its surface.

  • Type: Terrestrial Planet
  • Distance from Sun: 227.9 million km
  • Diameter: 6,779 km
  • Orbital Period: 687 Earth days
  • Moons: Phobos & Deimos
  • Surface Temp: −125°C to 20°C
  • Surface: Rocky, iron-oxide dust (red color)

Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest volcano in the solar system — about 3× the height of Mount Everest.
