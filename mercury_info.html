Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest to the Sun. With almost no atmosphere, temperatures swing wildly from scorching hot to freezing cold.

  • Type: Terrestrial Planet
  • Distance from Sun: 57.9 million km
  • Diameter: 4,879 km
  • Orbital Period: 88 Earth days
  • Moons: None
  • Surface Temp: −180°C to 430°C
  • Surface: Rocky and heavily cratered

A day on Mercury (sunrise to sunrise) lasts 176 Earth days — longer than its year!
